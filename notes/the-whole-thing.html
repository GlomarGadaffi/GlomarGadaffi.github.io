2026-06-23 · a map of the repos

the work·in orderby what it does

About two dozen repos, almost all of them small radio and networking projects on cheap microcontrollers. Most of it is firmware for the ESP32 family. This page groups them by what they do: a phone system, tools that listen to radio traffic, tools that transmit, private and resilient comms, and the supporting pieces. Most of these run in daily use.

01 · phone system

A SIP phone system on one board

A PBX that runs on a single ESP32-S3, a handset that registers to it, and the SIP stack underneath both. The three go together.

pocket-diala SIP PBX on one ESP32-S3. Register any SIP phone to it and make calls.→ + handseta push-to-talk SIP handset that registers to pocket-dial.→ resiprocatethe SIP, ICE, TURN, STUN and RTP stack both of the above use. vendored→
02 · receiving

Capturing and storing radio traffic

A Uniden scanner sends P25 trunk traffic over its serial port. These tools document that serial protocol, then parse, store, chart and search what comes out of it. The Meshtastic tool does the same job for mesh traffic.

bcd325p2-protocolthe serial command-protocol reference for the Uniden BCD325P2. The rest of this section depends on it.→ + splunk-addoningests the scanner's serial telemetry into Splunk and parses P25 and EDACS.→ + dashboarddecodes, charts and stores BCD325P2 telemetry.→ p25-trunk-loggerlogs a P25 trunked control channel and generates trunk-recorder configs.→ meshtappassive Meshtastic capture, sent to BigQuery over cellular MQTT.→
03 · transmitting and probing

Tools that transmit, scan or test

Wi-Fi and BLE security testing, the detector that catches those tests, wardriving, LoRa signal analysis, and voice over a plain FSK radio link.

The counterpart to marauder.→ wigle-wardrivera Wi-Fi and BLE wardriver for Wigle.net. Writes GPS-tagged logs to SD.→ esp32s3-mgmt-scannera passive 802.11 management-frame scanner. Receive only.→ ble-sign-beaconbroadcasts a short message as a BLE advertisement.→ lora-rf-toolkitLoRa signal analysis: frequency sweep, I/Q capture, chirp and parameter analysis.→ fsk-walkie-talkiea voice-over-FSK walkie-talkie using Codec2 and an SX1262 radio.→
04 · private and resilient comms

Encrypted and mesh communication

Voice over Tor, an SSH server small enough for an ESP32, and mesh networking that keeps working when the usual links don't. Two of the five are upstream projects I depend on rather than my own, and are marked as such.

tor-voiceencrypted voice over Tor: an in-process Arti onion service with Opus audio and the Noise protocol.→ glossha minimal SSH 2.0 server for ESP-IDF, built on PSA Crypto.→ esp-secure-transportan MIT and public-domain secure-transport stack for ESP-IDF. Not in active development. paused→ reticuluman encrypted mesh network over LoRa, packet radio, serial and TCP. vendored→ rfpartya dashboard for BLE and Wi-Fi telemetry. vendored→
