No Arduino, on purpose. deauth-detectorNotices when someone's kicking devices off 802.11, and tells the other nodes over LoRa. wigle-wardriverWardriver for Wigle.net. Logs Wi-Fi/BLE to SD with a GPS fix stapled to each one. esp32s3-mgmt-scannerPassive 802.11 management-frame scanner for the ESP32-S3. Listens, doesn't transmit. ble-sign-beaconShouts a short message as a BLE advertisement. The whole point is that it's seen.

mobile hotspot getting root on the thing in your pocket1

orbic-toolkitUniversal root-access & payload installer for the Orbic RC400L ARMv7 hotspot. Command injection via SetRemoteAccessCfg, then push any ARM binary as a managed init service. Network or USB path. Built on EFF rayhunter's exploit chain.

networking ads resolve to nowhere1

ESP32_AdBlocker_RebornA network-wide adblocker that fits on a dev board. Sinkholes ads and trackers at the DNS layer; the blocked ones get answered down in the Ethernet driver in ~1.8 ms, never touching lwIP.
